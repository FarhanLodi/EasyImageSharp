Derived files (all produced by gen_fonts.py with fontTools 4.60.1 and freetype-py / FreeType 2.13.2)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DejaVuSans-Subset.ttf        Subset of DejaVuSans.ttf: ASCII, Latin-1 and a few typographic, Greek and Cyrillic
                             characters; hinting removed; GPOS 'kern' and the legacy 'kern' table kept; an extra
                             (3,10) format 12 cmap subtable that also maps U+1D400 to the glyph of 'A'.
DejaVuSans-Bold-Subset.ttf   The same subset of DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf plus U+2665 and U+263A (for fallback tests).
DejaVuSans-Subset.otf        DejaVuSans-Subset.ttf converted to CFF outlines (quadratics become exact cubics, with
                             fractional 16.16 operands), renamed "DejaVu Sans CFF Test", with hand-made local and
                             global subroutines, a nested subroutine call and stem hints/hintmasks.
DejaVuSans-Subset-CID.otf    The same, rebuilt as a CID-keyed CFF ("DejaVu Sans CID Test") with two Font DICTs and
                             a format 3 FDSelect.
DejaVuSans-Subset.ttc        TrueType collection of the regular and bold subsets.
DejaVuSans-Subset.woff       WOFF 1.0 packaging of DejaVuSans-Subset.ttf.
DejaVuSans-Subset.woff2      WOFF 2.0 packaging of DejaVuSans-Subset.ttf with the glyf/loca and hmtx transforms.
reference.json               Independent expectations: metrics, names, cmap, advances, control bounds and GPOS /
                             legacy kerning read with fontTools, and unhinted glyph coverage rendered by FreeType
                             (FT_LOAD_NO_HINTING, FT_RENDER_MODE_NORMAL) at 12, 24 and 48 px.

Regenerating
------------
Download and unpack the archive above into a scratch directory, create a virtual environment with
fonttools==4.60.1, brotli and freetype-py, then run:

    python gen_fonts.py <archive>/dejavu-fonts-ttf-2.37/ttf

The files are written next to the script and their SHA-256 sums are printed. Generation is deterministic (the
source fonts' timestamps are kept), so a regeneration with the same tool versions reproduces these sums:
